  • harassment, threats, hate speech or content that incites violence;

You are responsible for your data, for what you send and for who you share your QR code with. You must be at least 13 years old, or the minimum age required in your country, to use the app.

How we enforce these terms

P2P Meseji has no servers: messages are end-to-end encrypted and travel directly between devices, so Mendelatech cannot read, store or delete them. What you can do:

  • Block: deleting a contact blocks them, so they can't write to you or add you again unless you add them again.
  • Report: use "Report" in the chat menu, or write to mendelatech@proton.me. We review every report. When it involves child sexual abuse material or a risk to someone's safety, we report it to the competent authorities and to organisations such as the NCMEC or the INHOPE hotlines, and we cooperate with law enforcement as the law requires.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.
